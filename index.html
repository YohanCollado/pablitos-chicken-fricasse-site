Our Story

Welcome to Pablito's Chicken Fricasse, where the vibrant flavors of Cuba meet the heart of our community. Founded by proud Cuban immigrants who came to the United States in the early 2000s, our restaurant is a labor of love and tradition. At Pablito's, we bring the warmth of a Cuban home into every dish we serve.

Our signature pollo fricasse is slow-cooked with rich spices, tender vegetables, and olives — every recipe carries a story. Come in, sit back, and savor the flavors that inspired our journey — and let us welcome you like family.

Line Cook (Cuban Cuisine Specialist)

Job Responsibilities

  • Prepare and cook traditional Cuban dishes, with emphasis on pollo fricasse.
  • Follow standardized recipes to maintain consistency and quality.
  • Keep workstations clean and follow food safety procedures.
  • Assist with inventory and receiving deliveries.

Qualifications

  • Experience as a line cook (preferred: Cuban or Latin cuisine).
  • Food safety knowledge; ServSafe or equivalent is a plus.
  • Bilingual (English/Spanish) helpful but not required.

Expectations

  • Availability for nights, weekends, and holidays.
  • Able to stand for long shifts and lift up to 40 lbs.
